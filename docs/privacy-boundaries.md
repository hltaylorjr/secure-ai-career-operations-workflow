# Privacy boundaries

## Public

- Approved professional biography and career facts
- Public website and professional-network links
- Privacy-sanitized résumé
- High-level workflow architecture
- Fictional examples and aggregate, non-identifying demonstrations

## Private

- Opportunity tracker and application status
- Employer communications
- Interview schedules and preparation notes
- Approved master and tailored résumés
- Interaction summaries and career-service correspondence

## Restricted

- Authentication and recovery information
- API and webhook secrets
- Caller numbers and raw communication metadata
- Billing, analytics, and production logs
- Private calendar, mailbox, and document contents

## Prohibited from the public repository

- Personal telephone numbers, personal email addresses, or home location
- Real applicant, recruiter, caller, advisor, or employer communications
- Medical, disability, legal, financial, or government-identification information
- Recordings, transcripts, recommendation letters, or academic records
- Private screenshots or authenticated portal content
- Production configuration capable of enabling misuse

## Retention principle

Collect the minimum necessary information for a defined career purpose, keep it only as long as needed, restrict access, and delete or anonymize it according to an approved retention process.
